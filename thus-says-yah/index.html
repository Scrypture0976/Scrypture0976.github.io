Daily reminder
A prompt to check in each morning.

Plain truth about reminders: this is a web app, and on iPhone a web app cannot push reminders reliably the way a store app can. What works: add this app to your Home Screen, open it each morning, and today's check-in will be waiting for you. If your browser allows notifications, this app will also try to send one at your chosen time while the app is open or recently used — but the Home Screen habit is the reliable path. Nothing here costs anything and nothing leaves your phone.

The full text of Yah Scriptures for all 113 books lives inside this app, so Ask and lookup work with no signal — tap any “Read the full chapter” link to open that chapter on yahscriptures.online.
